- **Solution**: taps in the solver's best order. **Best peak**: the fewest tray slots a perfect order needs; **margin** = tray size − best peak (always ≥ 1).
- **Bot taps**: the playtest bot (the in-game hint, as used by the autoplay recordings and e2e) plays the level from scratch; it must win.
- **Worst peak** (levels 1–10): the most tray slots ANY order of taps can fill. All ten are "cannot fail".
- **Random fail**: share of 300 games lost by tapping random free screws (a pessimistic forgiveness measure; people do much better). Bosses (every 10th) are the spikes, the next level drops back.
- **States / ms**: solver work per level (time limit 4000 ms).
